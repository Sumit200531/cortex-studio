01  Read

It reads like a tutor would

Paste a textbook chapter, a lecture transcript, or just ask a question. Cortex looks for the handful of ideas everything else depends on, and the details that hang from each of them.

Input: a topic, or up to ~6,000 words of notes

02  Connect

Ideas become neurons

Each concept becomes a node, and every relationship becomes a link you can follow. The structure a good student builds over weeks appears in seconds. Pick any node to get a plain-language explanation, or have the AI grow new branches from it.

Explain · Expand · Ask anything about the map

03  Recall

Rereading feels like learning. Recalling is.

Memory researchers call it the testing effect: pulling an answer out of your head strengthens it far more than reading it again. Cortex writes quizzes from your map and lights up each concept in amber once you've got it right.

Roediger & Karpicke, Psychological Science, 2006
